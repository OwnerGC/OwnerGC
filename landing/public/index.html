FAQ

Questions

How accurate is the estimate?

It's a planning number based on typical cost shares for each part of a build, adjusted for finish level and region. Real bids vary by area and season, so use it to plan, then confirm with local bids.

Is this for me if I'm hiring a builder?

The estimator works for anyone. The full app is built for people acting as their own general contractor, and for small builders and investors running several builds.

What will it cost?

The estimator stays free. Full tracking is planned at about $24 a month during your build, with a discount for founding members.
